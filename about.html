Precise, plain and independent.

Watches is an independent website about wristwatches.

We write for people choosing their first good watch, and for anyone who has wondered what a water rating or a “movement” really is. We explain the technical side carefully and without jargon, and we build small tools that measure and compare.

We do not sell watches, take payment for mentions or use affiliate links. Every guide is checked by a second editor and dated.
